## Words used in this file

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Advertised price** | The "from ₹X per person" price shown for a ready-made package on the website and in the app (this is what the agency's price list calls the package price). |
| **Our quote** | What our price calculator produces for the same trip: 2 adults, 3-star hotel, cheapest month, hotel breakfast only, **land only** (no flights). |
| **Land only** | Hotels, transfers, vehicle, sightseeing, meals. **Not** air tickets. |
| **Under** | Our quote is **lower** than the advertised price. A customer who builds the same trip themselves is shown a cheaper price than the package, which is a mistake in our favour of the customer and a loss to the agency. |
| **Over** | Our quote is **higher** than the advertised price. |
| **Package floor** | A safety rule: when a customer picks the ready-made package itself, the quote is never allowed to fall below the advertised price. It does **not** protect trips the customer builds themselves in that area. |
| **Per person** | All prices below are per person, for 2 adults sharing one room. |
