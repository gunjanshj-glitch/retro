Retro Holidays, in your pocket

A working mockup of the iPhone and Android app, for travellers and for Retro staff. Tap through the phone, or jump to a screen from the list. Flip Traveller / Staff to play both sides of one quote: ask for changes as the traveller, answer as staff, accept and pay.

Prototype only. Prices in the planner are a simple stand-in. The Rajasthan quote totals and categories are the real pricing engine's output for 2 adults over 11 nights; the conversation, v1 price and the split lines are illustrative. The map uses the real India map data and the real pin positions. Nothing here is sent anywhere.
