# 1. DESTINATION SUMMARY

**short_description:**

Koh Samui, Thailand is the largest island of the Gulf of Thailand's Samui archipelago, known for palm-fringed beaches, a relaxed resort culture, golden Buddhist landmarks, night markets, and day trips to the Ang Thong Marine National Park.

**travel_agent_summary:**

Koh Samui is the Gulf-coast counterpart to Phuket: a compact island of about one ring road, served by its own small airport (USM) that is dominated by Bangkok Airways, with an alternative overland-and-ferry route via Surat Thani and the Donsak pier. It suits couples, families, wellness seekers and first-time Thailand beach travelers. Base in Bophut or Mae Nam for a quieter, boutique feel, in Chaweng for energy and nightlife, in Lamai for a more relaxed mid-range beach town, or in Choeng Mon for a calmer upscale cove. Pair morning cultural stops (Big Buddha, Wat Plai Laem, Secret Buddha Garden) with afternoon beach time, and book a day trip to Ang Thong Marine National Park, which is closed to tours for part of the late-year monsoon. The island's best weather is roughly January to April, with the wettest weather around October to December, which is the reverse of Phuket's west-coast pattern and makes Samui a useful swap during Andaman monsoon months. A scooter is the common choice for locals, but private cars, songthaews and ride-hailing are safer for most clients.

# 6. IDEAL TRIP LENGTH

**minimum_nights:** 3

**ideal_nights:** 5

**maximum_first_visit:** 7

**Travel-agent interpretation:**

* **3 nights (4D3N):** The minimum for a first visit and a good beach add-on to a Bangkok or Phuket itinerary; enough for the Big Buddha and Wat Plai Laem, a Fisherman's Village evening and one half-day boat or waterfall excursion.
* **5 nights (6D5N):** The ideal classic stay; mixes beach time, an Ang Thong Marine Park day trip (outside the closure window), the south-coast waterfalls and rocks, a night market and a spa day at a relaxed pace.
* **7 nights (8D7N):** Recommended for full resort relaxation or a wellness retreat; allows splitting the stay between two coasts, an elephant sanctuary visit and several slow beach days.
* **7+ nights:** Not necessary for Samui alone; best for extended wellness stays or combining with Koh Phangan and Koh Tao.

**months:** January–April

**weather:** Warm, sunny and mostly dry (roughly 28–32°C) with calm seas; February is frequently described as the driest, sunniest month, and April is the hottest.

**months:** May–September

**crowd_level:** MEDIUM

## Wet Season

**months:** October–December

**weather:** Heaviest rainfall of the year on the island, with frequent downpours, cloud and rougher seas; November is widely cited as the wettest month.

**crowd_level:** LOW → MEDIUM

**recommended:** false

**reason:** Rain and swell disrupt boat trips, and Ang Thong Marine National Park is closed each year from 1 November to about 15 December (some calendars say 30 November); late December improves and holiday demand pushes prices back up.

# 9. BUDGET POSITIONING

**Overall cost level:**

MEDIUM → HIGH

**Budget traveler:** Workable with guesthouses and small bungalows in Lamai, Mae Nam or Nathon, local food stalls and night markets, shared songthaews, and free public beaches, temples and waterfalls; flights and island transfers are the main cost.

**Mid-range traveler:** Strong fit with 3-star and 4-star resorts in Chaweng, Lamai, Bophut and Mae Nam, ride-hailing and taxis, a mix of local restaurants and beachfront dining, and a paid day trip to Ang Thong Marine National Park.

**Premium traveler:** Excellent fit with 5-star beachfront resorts and pool villas in Choeng Mon, Bophut and the northern coast, private drivers, spa packages and private boat charters.

**Luxury traveler:** Good fit with private-villa estates on the island's hillsides and north-east coast, private speedboat and yacht days, chef-led dining and wellness retreats.

---

# 10. BUDGET STRATEGY

* Stay in Mae Nam, Lamai or Bophut rather than central Chaweng for lower accommodation rates while keeping access to beaches and night markets.
* Use night markets and local restaurants for meals rather than resort dining every evening.
* Book Ang Thong and other boat trips through licensed operators and check whether the national park fee is included, as it is often collected separately on the day.
* Combine free or low-cost sights, including the Big Buddha, Wat Plai Laem, Na Muang waterfalls and the Hin Ta Hin Yai rocks, into a single day with a private driver or shared taxi.
* Travel in the shoulder months (May to September) for lower hotel rates and reasonable weather.

**Where saving money is not recommended:**

* Renting a scooter without a motorcycle licence or riding experience; road accidents are a major risk on the island's hilly roads.
* Booking unlicensed boat operators or unverified diving and snorkeling trips.
* Using unmetered taxis without agreeing on the fare first.
* Skipping travel insurance and sun protection.
* Planning boat excursions in October to December without a flexible backup day.

# 11. ARRIVAL INFORMATION

Primary airports:

Samui Airport (IATA: USM): a small island airport owned and operated by Bangkok Airways; Bangkok Airways operates the direct flights from Bangkok (Suvarnabhumi and Don Mueang) and Phuket.

Overland and ferry alternative: fly to Surat Thani or take a train or bus to Surat Thani, then transfer to the Donsak (Don Sak) pier for a ferry to Samui; typical crossings are about 1 to 1.5 hours with Raja Ferry and Seatran, and Lomprayah offers a faster high-speed service.

# 12. AIRPORT TRANSFER LOGIC


# Family

Pre-booked private minivan from Samui Airport (USM) to the resort with child seats on request; transfer times are short on the island but vary by coast, typically under about 30 to 45 minutes.

# Honeymoon

Private sedan or SUV transfer arranged through the resort, with a meet-and-greet at the small open-air terminal and direct drop-off at the villa.

# Luxury

Resort-arranged private vehicle with a dedicated greeter, and optional private speedboat or charter transfers for island hopping.

# Budget

Shared minivan or airport taxi to the main beaches, or a songthaew or ride-hailing option where available; for the ferry route, book a combined bus-and-ferry ticket from Surat Thani to the Donsak pier.

**Travel-agent interpretation:**

Samui is circled by a ring road that connects Chaweng, Lamai, Nathon, Mae Nam, Bophut and Choeng Mon. Songthaews run along the main roads but are slow and fares are negotiated, so most visitors use hotel-arranged drivers, taxis or ride-hailing for door-to-door transfers. Scooters offer freedom but carry real accident risk, particularly in the rain and on steep hills. Ferries and longtail or speedboats from Nathon, Bophut and nearby piers connect the island to Koh Phangan, Koh Tao, Ang Thong Marine National Park and the Donsak mainland pier.

# 16.FIRST-TIME VISITOR PRIORITY

Priority 90–100:

* Ang Thong Marine National Park day trip by boat with kayaking, snorkeling and viewpoint stops (tours are suspended in the late-year monsoon closure).

Optional based on interests:

# 32. TRAVEL AGENT INSIGHTS

### What inexperienced planners often do wrong

* Plan boat trips and beach-heavy stays for October to December, when rain and swell are at their worst.
* Assume Ang Thong tours run all year, when they are suspended for part of November and December.
* Underestimate transfer times and costs between coasts and book Chaweng for clients who want quiet.
* Recommend scooters to inexperienced riders.

### What an experienced planner does differently

* Matches the area to the client profile: Chaweng for nightlife, Bophut for dining, Choeng Mon for privacy and Mae Nam for quiet.
* Uses private drivers for sightseeing days and keeps afternoons for rest.
* Uses Samui as a Gulf-coast alternative when the Andaman side is in monsoon.
* Checks flight availability on Bangkok Airways early, as the island has a single dominant carrier.

### Best planning rule

* Schedule temples and waterfalls in the morning and boat trips on early departures.
* Keep a flexible spare day for weather in wetter months.
* Confirm park fees, schedules and closure dates directly with operators.

# 35. FINAL TRAVEL AGENT VERDICT

**Best for:**

Beach and wellness lovers, couples, families and first-time Thailand travelers wanting a relaxed Gulf-coast island with easy resort infrastructure.

**Best duration:** 4–5 nights

**Best season:** January–April

**Best stay:** Choeng Mon or Bophut for couples and families, Chaweng for nightlife and Lamai or Mae Nam for value

**Best transport:** Pre-booked private transfers, taxis or ride-hailing, and licensed boat operators for day trips

**Must-do:**

* Visit the Big Buddha and Wat Plai Laem.
* Walk Fisherman's Village on a Walking Street evening.
* Take an Ang Thong Marine National Park day trip outside the closure window.
* Visit Na Muang Waterfall and the Hin Ta Hin Yai rocks.
* Visit the Samui Elephant Sanctuary for a no-riding experience.

**Avoid:**

* Visiting for beach and boat days in October to December.
* Renting a scooter without experience and a licence.
* Booking Chaweng for travelers who want quiet.
* Assuming Ang Thong tours run year-round.

**Biggest advantage:**

A relaxed, resort-led Gulf island with varied beach areas and good dry-season weather when the Andaman coast is wettest in other months.

**Biggest disadvantage:**

A single dominant airline at the airport, a wet end of the year and fewer large attractions than Phuket.
